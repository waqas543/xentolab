Privacy Policy

XentoLab app · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the XentoLab mobile app ("the app") uses and why. The app is published by XentoLab ("we", "us"). It lets you browse and search information about places, businesses and cities.

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use your location only when you open Nearby, and only after you give permission.
  • Your saved items and recent searches are stored on your phone, not on our servers.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We don't sell your information.

1. Information we process

Location. When you use the Nearby feature, the app asks for permission to use your location. If you allow it, the app sends your current coordinates to our server to find places around you. On Android you can choose to share only your approximate location, and the feature still works. We use your location only to answer that request. We don't build a location history, and the app does not use your location in the background. You can deny or withdraw permission at any time in your phone's settings. The rest of the app still works.

Searches and requests. When you search or open a listing, the app sends that request (for example, the text you searched for) to our server so it can return results. We don't link these requests to your identity.

Server logs. Like most online services, our server keeps basic technical logs. These include your IP address, the time of the request and the address that was requested, which can contain your search text or Nearby coordinates. We use the logs only to keep the service secure and working, keep them only for a limited time, and don't use them to identify or profile you.

Information stored on your phone. Items you save and your recent searches are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. You can clear them in the app, and they are removed when you uninstall it.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Account details, contacts, photos, files, or microphone or camera input.
  • Advertising identifiers. We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Payment information. The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

3. Actions that open other apps

When you tap to call, email, get directions, visit a website or share a listing, the app passes that information to the app you choose, such as your phone dialer, email client, maps app or browser. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

4. Business information shown in the app

Listings in the app (business names, addresses, phone numbers, websites and similar details) come from publicly available sources such as OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) and businesses' own public websites. If you represent a business and want a listing corrected or removed, or if a listing shows personal information about you, please contact us (see section 9) and we will review your request promptly.

5. Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with service providers that host and operate our servers on our behalf, and when the law requires it.

6. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we process.

7. Children

The app is a general business directory and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Turn location permission on or off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Clear your saved items and recent searches in the app, or uninstall the app to remove them.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your information. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, and we will honour those rights.
